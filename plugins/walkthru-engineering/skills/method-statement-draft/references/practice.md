## Case 1

Request: Draft a method statement for blockwork on level 1, villa project.

Input: IFC A-110 Rev B; spec 04 22 00 clause 3.2 bed joints 10 mm, clause 3.4 movement joints every 6 m. Site works 07:00–18:00. No HSE plan or template supplied. Subcontractor not named.

Expected: Sequence cites A-110 Rev B and clauses 3.2/3.4. Subcontractor and manpower To confirm. Every HSE control marked "To confirm with HSE". Permits listed only as Inference to confirm. Fallback template used and stated.

## Case 2

Expected: Work-at-height and hot-work controls cite HSE plan 6.3 and 7.1; hot work permit listed with source. Flood test 48 hours as consultant witness point citing clause 3.5. Other hazards without plan coverage marked To confirm with HSE.
