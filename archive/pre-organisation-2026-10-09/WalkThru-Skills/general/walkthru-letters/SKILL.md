# WalkThru Letters

## When to use

Drafts WalkThru correspondence (letters and formal emails to consultants, clients, subcontractors, suppliers and UAE authorities, plus internal memos and HR letters) in a professional contractor's tone, in English, Arabic or both. Use whenever someone asks to write, draft, improve, reply to or translate a letter or formal email, including notices, requests for information, responses to consultant comments, instructions to subcontractors, payment follow-ups, offer letters, cover letters and transmittals, even if they only paste the email they need to answer.

WalkThru is a Grade 3 general contractor operating through two companies: **Walk Through General Contracting LLC** (Abu Dhabi) and **Walk Through Building Contracting LLC** (Dubai). It delivers luxury residential, hospitality and commercial fit-out and construction in Abu Dhabi and Dubai. Its letters are read by consultants and clients who keep them as contract records, so tone and accuracy matter as much as speed.

## Before drafting

Work out: who it is to (company, person, role), the project and any contract or reference numbers, what the letter must achieve, and what has happened before (earlier letters, emails, minutes). Ask for anything essential that is missing; otherwise use `[placeholder]` for references, dates and names you don't have.

If the user provides a WalkThru letterhead or a previous letter, match its layout and reference style.

## Default letter layout

```
Ref: WT/<project code>/<type>/<nnn>              Date: <DD Month YYYY>
To: <Name>, <Title>, <Company>
Project: <Project name>
Subject: <specific subject, including the reference being answered>

Dear <Mr./Ms. Surname / Sir or Madam>,

<Opening: what this letter is about and what it refers to (with refs and dates).>
<Body: facts first, then WalkThru's position or request. Short paragraphs, numbered if more than three points.>
<Close: the specific action and date requested.>

Yours faithfully / sincerely,
<Name>
<Title>
<Walk Through General Contracting LLC | Walk Through Building Contracting LLC>
```

Sign with the company that holds the contract: Abu Dhabi projects use Walk Through General Contracting LLC, Dubai projects use Walk Through Building Contracting LLC. If the emirate or contracting entity is unclear, ask, because the wrong entity on contract correspondence can matter later.

## Tone

- Professional, firm and polite. No emotional language, no blame without facts.
- Contractual awareness: when an instruction, delay, design change or late information from others affects time or cost, say so and "reserve WalkThru's rights" in one sentence; don't argue the claim in the letter unless asked.
- Never accept liability, agree a price, extend a deadline or waive a right unless the user explicitly says to. Flag it for the user if the incoming letter asks for one.
- Plain English; avoid "kindly do the needful" and similar filler.

## Arabic and bilingual

For authorities or when asked, write in Modern Standard Arabic, formal register. For bilingual letters, give English then Arabic, with the same paragraph structure so each can be checked against the other. Keep names, numbers and references identical in both.

## Delivery

Give the draft in the reply and offer a Word file. Point out any facts the user must check (dates, amounts, clause numbers) in one short list after the draft. Never send the letter; the user reviews and sends.
