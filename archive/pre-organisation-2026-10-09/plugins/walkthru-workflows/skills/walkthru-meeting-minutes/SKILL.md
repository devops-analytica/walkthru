# WalkThru Meeting Minutes

## When to use

Turns rough notes, a Teams transcript or a voice-note transcript from a WalkThru meeting (client, consultant, subcontractor, site progress or internal management meetings) into formal Minutes of Meeting with decisions and an action table, plus a draft cover email. Use whenever someone shares meeting notes or a transcript, or asks for minutes, MOM, "write up the meeting", "action points from today's meeting", or "summarise this call", even if the notes are messy, partial or mixed English and Arabic.

Minutes are a contractual record on construction projects. Consultants and clients rely on them for instructions, agreed dates and responsibilities, so they must be accurate, neutral and complete. Messy notes in; a clean record out.

## Before writing

Pick out from the notes: project, meeting type, date, venue or Teams, attendees and their companies, and the previous MOM number if mentioned. If the project, date or attendees are missing, leave a clear `[to confirm]` placeholder rather than guessing.

If the user uploads a WalkThru MOM template, follow its layout exactly; the format below is the default.

## Format

```
MINUTES OF MEETING
Project: <name>                      MOM No.: <project code>-MOM-<nnn>
Meeting: <type, e.g. Weekly Progress Meeting No. 12>
Date / Time: <DD-MMM-YYYY, hh:mm>    Venue: <site office / Teams>
Attendees: <Name – Company – Role> (one per line)
Distribution: attendees + <others named in notes>

1. Review of previous minutes
2. <Agenda item>
   2.1 <Discussion point, written in the past tense, neutral>
   Decision: <if any>
...

ACTION ITEMS
| No. | Action | By (company/person) | Target date | Status |

Next meeting: <date/time/venue or [to confirm]>

These minutes will be considered accurate unless comments are received within 3 working days of issue.
```

## Writing rules

- Neutral, factual, past tense: "The Consultant advised that…", "WalkThru confirmed…". Record who said or instructed what, because responsibility matters later.
- Keep commitments exactly as stated. Never strengthen ("will try" stays "will try") or add dates nobody gave.
- Flag anything with time or cost impact (client instructions, scope changes, design changes, delays caused by others) with "(Note: potential time/cost impact)" so the commercial team can act.
- Translate Arabic content into English; keep names as written.
- One action per row, with one owner. Split "Contractor and Consultant to…" into two rows.

## Also provide

A 3-line draft cover email to distribute the minutes, and offer to add the action items to the walkthru-action-tracker register. Produce the minutes as a Word document when the user wants a file.
