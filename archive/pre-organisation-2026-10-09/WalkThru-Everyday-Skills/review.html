# Morning priorities and email triage

## Inputs and scope
Read [project-context.md](references/project-context.md). Use emails with sender, recipients/CC, timestamp, subject, body and message ID. Resolve the employee's role and query window from the request. Without a stated window, propose a clearly labelled window rather than claiming the last working day is known. Read full actionable threads, including relevant CC items; subjects alone are insufficient.

## Workflow
- Consolidate threads and duplicates while preserving message references. A newer resolution can change an earlier action; retain its history.
- Route by the employee's responsibility, not whether they appear in To or CC. Separate **Action today**, **Reply needed**, **FYI** and **No action**. Deadline, site stoppage, reported HSE issue, rejected submission and explicit decision requests warrant attention; a sender's title or the word urgent alone does not establish urgency.
- Turn each action into a concrete verb, owner and confirmed deadline. Label inferred ownership/date as To confirm. If the user is not the decision-maker, describe their coordination action rather than asking them to approve.
- Keep automated messages containing failed submissions or real deadlines; ignore only those with no useful action. Keep source totals reconcilable; report any uninspected messages.
- List the highest-priority items first, followed by remaining actions. Do not silently drop items to meet a length limit. Draft responses only when asked or useful; never claim they were sent.

## Deliverable
Use [brief-template.md](assets/brief-template.md). Include coverage, project, action, owner, due/basis, priority reason and source; a compact FYI section; and unresolved evidence. Preserve amounts/references exactly. Recommendations are separate from message instructions.

## Check before delivery
Confirm every priority can be traced to inspected content, resolved threads are not still chased, and deadlines without a basis remain unknown. The two synthetic requests in [practice.md](references/practice.md) cover ordinary triage and misleading thread updates.

# Action tracker and follow-up assistant

## Inputs
Read [project-context.md](references/project-context.md). Use the latest register plus new emails, minutes, site notes or a task list. Preserve the user's column structure where usable; otherwise use [register.csv](assets/register.csv). Establish the as-of date and working calendar for any date-based chase.

## Maintain the register
- Keep stable IDs. Match updates by project + reference + action scope, not title alone. Similar tasks on different sites remain separate. If matching is ambiguous, show the candidate update without merging it.
- Retain existing rows and IDs, including completed/cancelled history. Close only with explicit completion evidence; a follow-up email or partial completion is not closure.
- Due date means an agreed/supplied date. Separate a requested/proposed date and next chase date. Do not move an original deadline when someone asks for an extension; record Proposed due and approval pending.
- For new tasks, capture owner or Owner unknown, source, raised date where known, and status. Do not invent a responsible individual.
- Append changes to a separate Change log: ID, old value, new value, evidence, effective date. Return the full updated register, plus added/changed/closed counts.

## Follow-up output
Group active items into Overdue, Due today/within the requested window, Waiting on others and Due unknown. Sort confirmed due dates chronologically and avoid counting closed rows. State day basis; an item due today is not yet overdue. Keep no-date items visible without assigning fabricated deadlines.
Draft concise messages grouped by owner and project, with IDs, requested action and known dates. Suggested chase dates are recommendations. Nothing is sent by generating them.

## Deliverable and checks
Return the register, change log, chase list and missing-information list. XLSX: filters, frozen headers, date cells and separate Change log; otherwise separate CSV files. Check row/ID preservation, no cross-project merges and evidence for every closure/date change. Use [practice.md](references/practice.md) to rehearse conflicting updates.

# Meeting minutes and actions

## Inputs
Read [project-context.md](references/project-context.md). Use rough notes or a transcript, meeting metadata, previous minutes and an approved MOM template if supplied. Work with mixed English/Arabic notes; preserve original names/references and flag uncertain translations that change a commitment.

## Build the record
- Extract confirmed project, meeting date/time, attendees, venue and reference. Use To confirm for missing metadata; do not allocate an official MOM number or assume attendees from distribution recipients.
- Separate discussion, proposals, decisions and actions. Preserve attribution and modal language: “will try” is not “confirmed”; a proposed variation is not an approved change.
- Record disputed versions with both attributions. Note potential time/cost impact as a review point without deciding entitlement.
- Give each action an ID, scope, explicitly agreed owner and target date, evidence segment and status. For genuinely shared work, retain joint ownership or split only when distinct responsibilities are supported; do not invent one owner to complete a table.
- Carry forward prior actions using their IDs. Do not close them without completion evidence. Mark missing owners/dates for confirmation.
- Do not insert a deemed-acceptance statement or fixed comment period. Include one only from the supplied approved template/contract instruction, with its source; otherwise omit it.

## Deliverable
Use [minutes-template.md](assets/minutes-template.md) unless the company supplies a template. Return draft minutes, action table, short cover-email draft and a confirmation list. Keep internal evidence/uncertainties separate from the clean distribution draft when requested, but retain traceability in the reviewer copy.

## Check
Every decision and commitment must match the notes. Confirm currency/amounts, names and dates, no inferred acceptance and no missing material disagreements. [practice.md](references/practice.md) provides two rehearsal cases.

# Project correspondence assistant

## Inputs and routing
Read [project-context.md](references/project-context.md). Establish the purpose, recipient, contracting entity, project, supporting chronology, references, language and intended signatory. Confirm the entity from the contract or approved letterhead; geographic location alone is not evidence. Missing reference numbers can be marked To confirm without blocking a useful draft.

This skill formats the user's correspondence. For RFI technical findings, commercial calculations or contractual notice requirements, use the supplied findings rather than inventing an engineering/legal analysis.

## Draft
- Prefer the approved letterhead/template or a supplied previous example. Otherwise use [letter-template.md](assets/letter-template.md); keep document numbering provisional unless supplied by the register.
- Structure around the reference, verified facts, the intended position/request and a clear next action. Distinguish confirmed dates from requested response dates.
- Keep chronology and source references correct. If evidence conflicts, flag it to the reviewer instead of silently selecting a favourable account.
- Do not add admissions, acceptance of price/scope, waived rights, guaranteed completion or threats unless instructed and supported. A routine delivery chase does not need automatic rights-reservation wording.
- For notices/time-bar-sensitive correspondence, ask for the applicable clause, service method and trigger evidence if absent; prepare the factual draft and label notice compliance Unverified. Do not invent a notice period or assert compliance from a generic FIDIC assumption.
- For Arabic/bilingual output, align factual content, numbering, names, dates, amounts and obligations between versions. Flag wording whose translation changes legal effect.

## Deliver
Return the draft and a separate pre-issue checklist: entity/signatory, references, dates/amounts, attachments and unresolved facts. Include cited source chronology in the reviewer copy, not invented attachments. Generate DOCX if available or editable Markdown otherwise. The draft is not issued correspondence.

## Check
Verify no invented reference, entity or commitment, and no material change in meaning from the requested response. Rehearse with [practice.md](references/practice.md).

# Daily site report and next-day priorities

## Modes and inputs
Read [project-context.md](references/project-context.md). **Site report mode:** convert one engineer's raw notes into a DPR. **Operations digest mode:** consolidate existing DPRs across the supplied project list. Choose from the request; do not summarise away detail needed in a DPR.
Use report date, location/workfront, activities, quantities/units, manpower by trade, equipment, deliveries, inspections, HSE/quality entries, instructions and next-day needs. Compare with a supplied plan/look-ahead only. Show report date and review date separately; mixed-date reports must be grouped by date, not summed into one day.

## Analyse
- In site mode, organise reported facts using [site-report-template.md](assets/site-report-template.md). Unknown weather, incident status or quantities remain Not reported, not guessed.
- In digest mode, report received/expected projects and missing reports; without an expected list, say coverage unknown. Identify stopped/delayed work, missing materials/drawings, equipment failures, reported HSE issues, failed inspections, NCRs and consultant instructions.
- Calculate manpower gap = actual minus planned by the same trade, site and date. No plan means no measured shortfall. Do not impose an unapproved 10% alarm threshold. Progress variance requires comparable scope/measurement; do not infer percentages from narrative.
- Distinguish No incidents reported from Verified no incidents. Keep HSE findings visible rather than evaluating whether the site is safe.
- Record workfront and stated impact. Schedule criticality needs a programme link; label unmatched links Inference to confirm.
- Suggest resource moves only from evidenced released capacity of the right trade, on the available date, subject to qualifications/access/transport constraints. Never deploy workers or treat a future release as available today.
- Next-day needs refer to the next working/reporting date established by the user; if the calendar is unknown, label the intended date rather than assuming it.

## Deliver and check
Return DPR/digest, issue/action table, comparable manpower table, instructions requiring review and next-day arrangements. Use [manpower.csv](assets/manpower.csv) if no company table is supplied. Separate reported facts from suggested actions and unknown owners. Check duplicate reports, dates, trade matching, units and missing evidence. See [practice.md](references/practice.md).

# Submittal, RFI and inspection follow-up

## Inputs
Read [project-context.md](references/project-context.md). Accept shop drawing/material/method-statement submittal logs, RFI logs and WIR/inspection registers, with the project status legend and optional look-ahead. Establish as-of date, applicable revision, actual submission/response date, explicit due date or confirmed review rule/calendar. Use [engineering-register.csv](assets/engineering-register.csv) as fallback.

## Reconcile and classify
- Group by project + document reference + type; retain each revision. A current pending revision must not inherit an approval from a superseded revision. Confirm when the applicable revision is ambiguous.
- Interpret codes using the supplied legend, not a universal A–D assumption. Unknown codes remain Unmapped. Separate review result from whether site execution is permitted.
- **Our action:** not submitted, revise/resubmit, comments not incorporated, failed WIR awaiting rectification or RFI follow-up assigned to WalkThru.
- **External action:** pending consultant/client/authority response or inspection. Record who actually holds the next action, rather than classifying every RFI as consultant-owned.
- **Confirmed overdue:** open item past an evidenced due date under the applicable revision. When only a confirmed working-day rule is given, require a complete working calendar before calculating due dates. Otherwise show Due unknown and submission age in calendar days. Keep provisional internal targets out of contractual-overdue totals.
- A requested WIR date without a confirmed inspection appointment is Pending requested inspection, not a missed consultant commitment. Failed inspection does not equal approval after resubmission.
- Link pending records to scheduled work via explicit activity/location references. Similar title/trade matches are Inference to confirm. State upcoming-work horizon used (default 21 calendar days, adjustable); do not call an item critical without schedule evidence.

## Deliver
Return current-revision summary, our-side list, external-side list, confirmed overdue list, blockers and unmapped/missing evidence. Count documents and revision rows separately. Include draft grouped chase emails with references, dates and factual impacts. Produce a proposed register update/change log if requested; generating a local proposal does not change the live log.

## Check
No inherited approvals, fabricated due dates or conflated RFI/WIR statuses. Cite each finding to a row/page. Use [practice.md](references/practice.md) to test revision and calendar boundaries.

# Materials and delivery tracker

## Inputs
Read [project-context.md](references/project-context.md). Use material requests, requisitions/RFQs, quotations, POs/LPOs, approval/fabrication logs, supplier commitments, GRNs/delivery notes, stock/issues/returns and a site look-ahead. Use [materials-register.csv](assets/materials-register.csv) if no company register exists. Confirm as-of date, project, item/specification, unit and workfront.

## Reconcile the chain
- Keep stages distinct: Requested → RFQ → Selected/approval pending → Ordered → Supplier acknowledged → Fabrication/shipped → Received → Accepted. A quotation is not an order; a PO date is not a delivery commitment; received/rejected goods are not usable stock.
- Match project + item/specification + PO line + unit. Keep partial deliveries separate and preserve delivery-note IDs. Deduplicate re-sent notes; reconcile cancellations/returns and conflicting revisions without silently overwriting them.
- Calculate accepted receipts net of returns; usable on-site balance = opening usable stock + accepted receipts − issued/consumed − returns, for the same period and unit. Do not subtract returns twice if a supplied accepted figure is already net. Unknown opening stock or movements means Usable balance unknown, not zero.
- Compare required quantity with known usable/unallocated quantity on the required date. Record future accepted/confirmed receipts separately from today's stock. No unit conversion, alternative product equivalence or allocation across sites without a confirmed basis.
- Separate Required-on-site date, Requested delivery, Supplier confirmed delivery and Actual receipt. Flag late confirmed dates, missing commitments and prerequisites (technical approval, payment, fabrication, permits). Quoted lead time needs its trigger date and calendar; otherwise an expected arrival is provisional.
- Describe programme exposure from the supplied activity link. A shortage with no required date is a quantity concern with date unknown, not proven delay. Do not place/cancel orders or promise suppliers payment.

## Deliver
Return stage register, confirmed late deliveries, missing commitments, known shortages/unknown balances, prerequisite actions and grouped supplier follow-up drafts. Preserve references and source-based dates; use the company escalation thresholds if supplied. Separate total ordered, received, accepted and usable quantities.

## Check
Confirm no duplicate receipts, no rejected goods counted as usable, no double-counted returns, mixed units or project stock. Rehearse with [practice.md](references/practice.md).

# Invoice and payment-request pre-check

## Inputs and scope
Read [project-context.md](references/project-context.md). Obtain invoice/payment request, PO/LPO/SO, accepted GRNs/measurement support, prior billing ledger, applicable terms and tax treatment. Confirm vendor/project/entity/currency independently. Uploaded aliases need documentary confirmation. Distinguish a material invoice from subcontract progress claims, advances and credit notes.

## Checks
- PO existence/identity and authority evidence; item specification, unit and line allocation; invoice quantity and rate; arithmetic; cumulative supported deliveries/previous billing; duplicates; transaction-specific tax; contract deductions and payment-request support.
- For material quantities, remaining supported = max(0, min(approved ordered, cumulative accepted delivered) − previous booked quantity). Reconcile returns/credits before applying this formula; preserve explicitly evidenced zero history. Missing history is not zero.
- Calculate a line discrepancy once: claimed amount minus supported amount. Do not add overlapping quantity and rate differences. Aggregate invoice totals once even when it has multiple exception rows. Keep currencies/entities separate.
- Duplicates: check vendor + invoice identifier against the supplied ledger, then flag same-date/amount matches as Suspected duplicate to investigate. State ledger coverage; do not call it fraud or company-wide clearance.
- Confirm tax treatment/rate, taxable base, rounding and TRN requirements from supplied evidence. TRN presence does not prove registration validity. No universal 5% assumption.
- Subcontract work needs certified measurement, cumulative previous certification, variations and applicable retention/advance/contra-charge reconciliation. The material helper below does not certify it. Split positive invoices and credit-note reconciliations rather than forcing negative values through the helper.

## Deterministic material review
Read [operation-checks.md](references/operation-checks.md). Normalize evidence into JSON with source references and run `python scripts/operation_checks.py invoice input.json --out result.json`. Do not set history_complete or delivery_complete true without the corresponding reconciled records. Confirm helper rounding/tax assumptions fit the transaction; otherwise show a separately reviewed calculation. Read its results alongside the documents: it cannot verify authority, authenticity, terms or certification.

## Deliver
Use [checks.csv](assets/checks.csv) and [exceptions.csv](assets/exceptions.csv). Each check is Pass, Exception, Not checked or Not applicable, with source. Classify invoices as **Hold—exceptions**, **Incomplete review—missing evidence**, or **No exceptions in supplied evidence—manager review required** only when all applicable checks are complete, including checks outside the helper.
Return claimed/supportable amounts only within the checked scope, remaining evidence, and draft vendor/internal queries. An incomplete review must never receive approval wording. Generating this report does not approve, book or pay an invoice.

## Check
Verify source extraction, previous billing/accepted receipts, totals once, unknowns and required checks outside the helper. See [practice.md](references/practice.md) and the synthetic normalization example [invoice.json](assets/invoice.json).
