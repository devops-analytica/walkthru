# Meeting minutes and actions

## Inputs
Read [project-context.md](references/project-context.md). Use rough notes or a transcript, meeting metadata, previous minutes and an approved MOM template if supplied. Work with mixed English/Arabic notes; preserve original names/references and flag uncertain translations that change a commitment.

## Build the record
- Extract confirmed project, meeting date/time, attendees, venue and reference. Use To confirm for missing metadata; do not allocate an official MOM number or assume attendees from distribution recipients.
- Separate discussion, proposals, decisions and actions. Preserve attribution and modal language: “will try” is not “confirmed”; a proposed variation is not an approved change.
- Record disputed versions with both attributions. Note potential time/cost impact as a review point without deciding entitlement.
- Give each action an ID, scope, explicitly agreed owner and target date, evidence segment and status. For genuinely shared work, retain joint ownership or split only when distinct responsibilities are supported; do not invent one owner to complete a table.
- Carry forward prior actions using their IDs. Do not close them without completion evidence. Mark missing owners/dates for confirmation.
- Do not insert a deemed-acceptance statement or fixed comment period. Include one only from the supplied approved template/contract instruction, with its source; otherwise omit it.

## Deliverable
Use [minutes-template.md](assets/minutes-template.md) unless the company supplies a template. Return draft minutes, action table, short cover-email draft and a confirmation list. Keep internal evidence/uncertainties separate from the clean distribution draft when requested, but retain traceability in the reviewer copy.

## Check
Every decision and commitment must match the notes. Confirm currency/amounts, names and dates, no inferred acceptance and no missing material disagreements. [practice.md](references/practice.md) provides two rehearsal cases.
